As a Paragon Science and Technology Policy Fellow assigned to the Arizona Commerce Authority (Arizona Space Commission), I:

- reviewed global space-economy trends, supply-chain vulnerabilities and research commercialisation pathways;
- led editorial oversight of a multi-author policy report with contributions from six fellows;
- redesigned the Foresight AZ: Space Tech Report across satellite manufacturing, launch vehicles, space tourism, defence and mining;
- interviewed the Mayor of Yuma, Tech Launch Arizona directors and UArizona Space Institute leadership;
- wrote fact sheets and policy briefs on supply-chain localisation, including semiconductors for space-resistant microchips, and on Yuma spaceport development for small-satellite payloads.
