**Recognition.** First place and a cash prize in the "Chip In for Arizona" Semiconductor Branding Challenge, hosted by EMD Electronics, SEMI, the Arizona Commerce Authority and the Greater Phoenix Economic Council.

**My role: brand engineer.** I developed the name, brand story, vision statement, rollout strategy and impact plan, and presented live at the SEMI Arizona Breakfast Forum to a panel that included the Governor of Arizona, the Mayor of Phoenix, industry leaders, executives and educators.

**The idea.** Silicon Diyógí reimagines the state's identity through a culturally grounded, visually rich and inclusive narrative, fusing Indigenous weaving metaphors with the semiconductor supply chain. The presentation included original design work, Navajo cultural references, ecosystem mapping, and an audience participation experience with individually printed cards.
