Over 15 hours a week in Dr W. Wang's spintronics lab at the University of Arizona, I analysed magnetic switching in nano-scale devices to help improve magnetic sensor and memory design, detect defects in magnetic layers, and evaluate how materials and stack structure affect performance.

- **TMR and MOKE testing** of magnetic switching behaviour.
- **LabVIEW sub-VI** that automated voltage acquisition during PtMg spin-orbit torque magnetic tunnel junction testing.
- **XRD and XRR** characterisation of how thickness and density affect TMR performance.
- **Nanopillar microscopy** after etching, to check structural integrity and fabrication consistency.
