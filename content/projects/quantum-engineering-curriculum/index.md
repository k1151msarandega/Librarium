**QuantrolOx (from July 2026).** Two introductory books for the Foundations 1 course of a quantum engineering training programme: *Mathematics* (vectors and complex numbers) and *Basic Circuit Theory* (DC and AC circuits, capacitors, inductors, impedance and resonance). Each topic uses worked examples, analogies and short Context sections that link it to quantum hardware.

**WISER (from December 2025).** A course on AI for quantum hardware design and autonomous experimentation: AI-accelerated device characterisation, Bayesian optimisation for quantum tuning, and closed-loop DBTL workflows, for graduate students and industry practitioners. In the outline and expert-engagement phase.
