- **Laser integration.** Custom-wavelength laser diodes integrated into fibre-coupled optical systems using CAD, datasheets and vendor specifications, giving fully aligned prototypes for a multi-system deployment.
- **Optomechanics.** Optical mount layouts and breadboard configurations in CAD, using assembly constraints to simulate alignment tolerances.
- **Mirror deflection.** Gravitational deflection analysis of precision mirrors in cloud-based FEA, comparing 6061-T6 aluminium, BK7 glass and fused silica, to guide material selection.
- **Materials.** Statistical Design of Experiments, including Taguchi methods, to optimise UV-curable optochemical formulations.
- **Procurement and market research.** Supplier engagement and RFQs for R&D components, and market research for an edge-deployable, privacy-focused offline AI platform.
