**Recognition.** Best Research Project Award, WISER 2025 Quantum Hackathon, for a PDE solver for CFD. Continued as a Quantum Solutions Research Fellow at WISER from December 2025.

**The work.**

- A quantum-amenable operator framework that maps PDE operators into matrix forms compatible with tensor-network and quantum-circuit representations.
- Feasibility checks for running CFD operators on near-term quantum hardware, with prototype implementations and operator-level benchmarking.
- A unified PDE solver prototype that combines classical CFD baselines with quantum-inspired operator decompositions and tensor-network reductions.
- Generalised Madelung and Hamiltonian-Schrödinger formulations of fluid dynamics, to find quantum-ready representations of nonlinear PDEs.

A peer-reviewed manuscript on quantum-accelerated PDE workflows is in preparation.
